Questions we get

Do I need to book?

No. Walk in during opening hours. Booking only saves you the wait, and the tag above is your booking in practice.

What if you find something extra?

We message you a photo and a price. Nothing extra is fitted until you say yes.

Do you work on e-bikes?

Yes for brakes, gears and wheels. We don’t open the motor or battery; for those we point you to the maker’s service centre.

How long is the guarantee?

Six months on our workmanship. If the same fix fails, we redo it free.
